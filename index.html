Three wedges into Denmark

01 · Urgency

Compliance

Article 50 has been live since 2 August. Every Danish voicebot now needs disclosure and machine-readable provenance. A dated obligation with a named owner, usually the DPO or CISO.

02 · Politics

Sovereignty

Denmark is actively cutting US platform dependence. Copenhagen's Microsoft licence bill rose 72% in five years. Around 99% of Danish AI still runs on Microsoft or OpenAI. A European vendor is a real answer.

03 · Proof

The untouched channel

Norlys already automates 82% of chats. Danish contact centres have proven text automation works. Voice, the expensive channel, is barely touched. The ROI case is already won internally.
